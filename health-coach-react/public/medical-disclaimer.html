Medical Disclaimer

Health Coach is wellness guidance, not medical care.

Meal, workout, reminder, and progress features are educational planning tools. They do not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

Last updated: May 3, 2026

Professional Review

Consult a qualified clinician or dietitian before following a plan if you are under 18, pregnant, managing diabetes, taking medication, have a history of disordered eating, or have heart, kidney, blood pressure, injury, or other medical concerns.

Safety Limits

The app should not be used for extreme calorie restriction, rapid weight-loss promises, overtraining, or supplement and medication advice. Stop exercise that causes pain, dizziness, chest pain, or shortness of breath and seek professional help.

Dietitian Approval

Plans that require dietitian approval should not be treated as ready to use until the dietitian has reviewed and approved them.
