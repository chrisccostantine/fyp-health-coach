Privacy Policy

Your health data stays tied to your planning experience.

Health Coach uses the information you provide, including profile details, goals, meal plans, workout plans, and calendar preferences, to generate personalized coaching and scheduling recommendations.

Last updated: April 30, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect account details such as your name, email address, and login credentials, along with the fitness, nutrition, and scheduling data you enter into the app.

  • Profile data such as age, height, weight, sex, and activity level
  • Goal preferences, exercise preferences, and diet preferences
  • Generated meal, workout, and calendar plan data
  • Connected calendar authorization tokens when you choose to link Google Calendar

How We Use Information

We use your information to create and update personalized daily plans, sync plan items to your calendar when you authorize it, improve app reliability, and support your account.

Google Calendar Access

If you connect Google Calendar, Health Coach uses Google Calendar permissions only to create, update, and remove events that mirror your meal and workout plan. We do not use your calendar data for advertising or unrelated profiling.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by hosting, database, and infrastructure providers that help operate the app.

Data Retention and Security

We retain your data while your account remains active or as needed to operate the service. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored account, plan, and calendar token data.

Your Choices

You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time from the app. You may also contact us to request account support or deletion assistance.

Questions about privacy can be sent through the support page linked above.
