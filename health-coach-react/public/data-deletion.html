Data Deletion Policy

You can delete your account and health data.

Health Coach includes account deletion controls so users can remove stored profile, plan, progress, message, update, and calendar data tied to their account.

Last updated: May 3, 2026

How to Delete

Sign in, open your dashboard account area, and choose Delete Account. The app will remove account records and associated health planning data.

Export First

Users can export their stored data from the Privacy section before deletion. Deleted data may not be recoverable.

Support

If you cannot access your account, contact support from the Support page with the email address associated with your account.
